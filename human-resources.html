Human Resources Consulting

We provide HR support services to assist organisations in building and managing effective project teams — from recruitment through to day-to-day workforce coordination.

What We Offer

HR Support Tailored for Technical Teams

Managing people in technical and engineering environments requires a specialist touch. Our HR services are designed specifically for project-driven organisations in the geoscience, mining, infrastructure, and environmental sectors.

🔍

Recruitment & Staffing Support

We assist organisations in sourcing, screening, and placing the right talent for technical and engineering projects. Our recruitment support covers specialist geoscience roles, field technicians, project managers, and supporting staff — ensuring your team has the right skills at the right time.

🌱

Talent Management & Workforce Coordination

Beyond hiring, we help clients retain and develop their people. Our talent management support covers workforce planning, skills gap analysis, performance frameworks, and coordination of multi-disciplinary project teams to ensure projects are delivered efficiently and on schedule.

📋

HR Administration & Compliance Support

We provide practical HR administration support — from employment documentation and onboarding processes to compliance with South African labour law and reporting requirements. Let us handle the administrative burden so your team can focus on delivery.

🤝

Project Team Coordination

Efficient project delivery depends on well-coordinated teams. We support clients in structuring project teams, defining roles and responsibilities, and maintaining communication and workflow across technical disciplines — from field staff to senior specialists.

Our Approach

How We Work With You

A straightforward, four-step process from initial brief to ongoing support.

1
Understand Your Needs

We start with a detailed brief to understand your organisation, project scope, and team requirements.

2
Develop a Plan

We propose a tailored HR support plan — whether that's a recruitment drive, team structure, or compliance review.

3
Execute & Coordinate

We implement the plan alongside your team, managing timelines and ensuring every step is handled professionally.

4
Ongoing Support

Our relationship doesn't end at placement. We provide continued coordination and HR support for the life of your project.
